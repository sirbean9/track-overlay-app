How it works

Import, review, export

STEP 1

Import

Drag in a session's video files. The track is recognised, and laps, sectors and corners are found for you.

STEP 2

Review

Compare any two laps and arrange the overlay on the video.

STEP 3

Export

Render the best lap, any lap or the whole session, encoded on your graphics card.
